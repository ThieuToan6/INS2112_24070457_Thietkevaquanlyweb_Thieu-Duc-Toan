Welcome to Art Club

Art Club is a welcoming place for anyone who enjoys exploring creativity. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced artist, you can share ideas, learn new techniques, and create artwork in a friendly, supportive environment.

Members can experiment with different materials and styles, from colorful paintings to digital illustrations. We also hold creative challenges and group projects, giving everyone a chance to learn from one another and proudly share their work.

Digital

Digital art lets members create, edit, and refine their ideas using a laptop, computer, or smartphone. Drawing apps offer helpful tools such as layers, adjustable brushes, and undo options, so artists can experiment without worrying about making mistakes.

From character illustrations and posters to photo edits and graphic designs, there are many ways to get started. Members share tips about their favorite apps and learn how to turn a first sketch into a finished digital piece.

Application

  1. Adobe Photoshop
  2. Clip Studio Paint
  3. Adobe Illustrator

Tradition

Traditional art gives members the chance to create with their hands and explore the feel of real materials. Paper, fabric, and canvas each offer a different surface, while pencils, paints, and markers help bring an idea to life.

Working with traditional materials can be a relaxing way to practice observation, color, and composition. Members are encouraged to try different techniques, make creative choices, and enjoy the process as much as the finished artwork.

Tradition material

  • Paper
  • Fabric
  • Canvas
