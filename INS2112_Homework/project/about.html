Music Club

Music Club was founded by a group of students who share a love for music. We welcome members of all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced musicians. Our club regularly organizes rehearsals, workshops, and small performances. Our goal is to build a friendly community where everyone can learn and enjoy music together.

Our Activities

  • Weekly rehearsals
  • Music workshops and masterclasses
  • Night concerts
  • Question and answer sessions with everyone
  • Guide for members on playing musical instruments

Anyone who is currently a student at our school is welcome to join the club and take part in all of these exciting activities with us.

These activities will spark an interest in music, deepen understanding of instruments such as the guitar, bass, and traditional instruments and provide insight into popular music culture,
while also allowing people to connect with others who share their passion.
